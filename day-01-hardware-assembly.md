## Work Completed

1. Soldered the connections for the TB6612FNG motor driver module.
2. Mounted the N20 DC gear motors and wheels onto the robot chassis.
3. Tested the motors using a direct battery supply.

## Understanding

1. When the polarity across a DC motor's terminals is reversed, the motor changes its direction of rotation from clockwise to anticlockwise or vice versa.

2. The motor driver uses an H-bridge circuit to control the direction of motor rotation. By changing the direction control inputs, the motor driver can make the motor rotate clockwise or anticlockwise.
